# Age rating

App Store Connect → App Information → Age Rating → Edit.

These answers follow the age-rating questionnaire Apple introduced in 2025 (ratings 4+, 9+,
13+, 16+, 18+). The wording in App Store Connect may differ slightly; answer by meaning.
Every answer is based on what build 1.0.2 (17) does, not on the web app.

**Expected rating: 4+.** Not Made for Kids.

## Features

| Question | Answer | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Parental controls | No | None in the app |
| Age assurance | No | Accounts are issued by the unit to vetted adult members |
| Unrestricted web access | **No** | The only web view shows `yahpz.com/privacy`; there is no address bar or free browsing |
| User-generated content | **No** (judgment call, see below) | Records are internal unit paperwork, not content published to other users |
| Social media | No | Not a social network; no profiles, feeds or following. App Store Connect had this as No already (29 Oct) |
| Messaging and chat | No | No in-app messaging. The feedback form goes to the unit's admins; the contacts tab hands off to the phone dialer or WhatsApp |
| Advertising | No | No ads, no ad SDKs |

**On user-generated content.** Members document events (text, odometer readings, plates,
photos) and send feedback. Only authorized staff of the same unit see these records, as
operational paperwork. Nothing is posted publicly or to a feed. Answering Yes would bring in
guideline 1.2 (filtering, reporting and blocking), which is meant for social apps. If App
Review disagrees, switch to Yes. It should not change the rating.
